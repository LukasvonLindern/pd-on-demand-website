The starting point

People development is usually well intended. It is rarely measurable.

Many companies invest in training and programmes without knowing where their people actually stand. Without that baseline there is no direction, and development comes down to chance.

On top of that, most providers sell one instrument rather than a solution. On the right are the three sentences we hear most often in first conversations, and how we deal with them.

What we hear
I do not know which instrument fits our situation.

Our answer: We are not tied to any provider. In the first conversation we clarify what you need and recommend the instrument that fits, including when that is the cheaper one.

What we hear
I have to justify this internally.

Our answer: You get results your leadership team understands. Specific development areas and specific next steps, not personality prose.

What we hear
I do not want a vendor who sells me a tool.

Our answer: We do not hand over a tool. We stay with the whole process, from choosing the instrument to the debriefing and the follow-up eight weeks later.
