Why PD on Demand

Assessment only helps when people can do something with it.

Many reports describe people accurately and still remain unused. We take a different view: the result is the beginning of the work, not the end. The debriefing creates a shared understanding, and the development plan turns that understanding into action.

The person assessed receives the full report first. What is shared with the commissioning organisation is agreed transparently in advance. That is the basis for trust and for meaningful development.
